Our Sustainability Promise

Mission

“La Couronne’s mission is to provide consumers with high-quality, safe, and sustainable false eyelashes made from renewable pineapple leaf fiber (PALF). The enterprise is dedicated to enhancing beauty while reducing ecological impact, supporting local agricultural communities, and fostering inclusive growth. Through continuous innovation, strict quality assurance, and transparent business practices, La Couronne strives to empower individuals, strengthen industry trust, and achieve long-term sustainability in the false eyelashes sector.”

Vision

“By 2030, La Couronne envisions becoming a recognized leader in the sustainable false-eyelash industry, driven by innovative cluster false eyelashes made from pineapple leaf fiber (PALF). Through its commitment to superior product quality and environmental sustainability, La Couronne seeks to set new standards in sustainable beauty innovation, foster consumer confidence, promote responsible beauty consumption, and contribute to the long-term advancement of a more sustainable cosmetics industry.”

Notes on Beauty & Better

Materials · 4 min read

Why Pineapple Leaf Fiber?

Every pineapple harvest leaves behind mountains of leaves. We spin that agricultural waste into a soft, breathable fiber — no new farmland required.

Read More

PALF is extracted from the long leaves left after the fruit is harvested, then washed, dried and refined. Because the raw material already exists, the process uses far less water and land than conventional synthetic lash fibers. The result is a lash that feels lighter on the eye and returns something to the farmers who grow it.

Ritual · 3 min read

Applying Cluster Lashes in Five Minutes

Cluster lashes are the friendliest way to build volume at home — a few small fans, a little glue, and a mirror.

Read More

Curl your natural lashes, then apply a thin line of adhesive along the base of a cluster. Place it just under your lash line, starting at the outer corner and working inward. Give each cluster ten seconds to set before adding the next. Finish with a spoolie to blend everything together.

Sustainability · 5 min read

Our Journey to Zero Waste Packaging

From recyclable cartons to soy-based inks, here is how we are shrinking the footprint behind every La Couronne set.

Read More

Our boxes are made from recycled board and printed with low-impact inks, and the lash trays are designed to be reused as travel cases. We also work with our partner farms to compost leftover leaf pulp, closing the loop on the material that started it all.
